LESSON: Excessive Controls

Managing too many controls on a single form can clutter your user interface and make your application hard to maintain. 

Best Practices for UI Design:
- Grouping: Use Panel or GroupBox controls to organize related elements logically.
- Reusability: Instead of creating 50 individual labels, use loops or shared components where possible.
- Visibility Control: Hide or show sections dynamically rather than crowding the screen all at once.
